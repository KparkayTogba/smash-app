01
Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate Smash:

  • Email address — used exclusively for passwordless OTP authentication. Never for marketing.
  • Profile information — display name, age, bio, and personality prompt answers.
  • Photos — images you upload, shown only to other Smash users.
  • Messages — in-app messages to deliver the messaging feature.
  • Usage data — non-identifiable app usage to help us improve.
  • Device information — model, OS version, app version for compatibility.
  • Reports and blocks — records for safety enforcement.
02
How We Use Your Data
  • To create and manage your Smash account
  • To authenticate you via email OTP
  • To display your profile to other users
  • To enable personality-based discovery and matching
  • To deliver real-time messaging
  • To process safety reports and blocks
  • To send login codes — nothing else
  • To fix bugs and improve performance
  • To comply with applicable laws
03
How We Share Your Data

We do not sell your personal information. Limited sharing in these scenarios only:

  • With other users — your profile, photos, bio, and prompts are visible to other Smash users.
  • With service providers — Supabase for hosting, under strict confidentiality.
  • For legal compliance — if required by law or to protect user safety.
04
Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Authentication uses email one-time passcodes — no passwords ever created or stored. Backend powered by Supabase with row-level security across all tables.

05
Deletion & Retention

We retain your data while your account is active. Delete via Profile → Settings → Delete Account and we permanently remove your profile, photos, messages, prompts, and credentials within 30 days. This cannot be undone.

06
Your Rights
  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and all data
  • Withdraw consent to optional data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

Contact us at libdon4life@yahoo.com.

07
Children's Privacy

Smash is exclusively for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If we become aware a minor has registered, we delete their data immediately.

08
Community Safety

Smash does not tolerate harassment, exploitation, abusive behavior, or inappropriate content. Users can report or block others at any time. All reports reviewed within 24 hours. Violations may result in permanent removal.

09
Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We update the effective date above when we do. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
