01

Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate Smash as a personality-first social dating app:

  • Email address — used exclusively for passwordless OTP authentication. Never used for marketing without consent.
  • Profile information — your display name, age, bio, and personality prompt answers that you voluntarily provide.
  • Photos — images you upload to your profile. Stored securely and shown only to other Smash users.
  • Messages — in-app messages sent between users to deliver the messaging feature.
  • Usage data — general, non-identifiable app usage to help us improve the experience.
  • Device information — device model, OS version, and app version for compatibility and debugging.
  • Reports and blocks — records of users you have reported or blocked for safety enforcement.
02

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your Smash account
  • To authenticate you via email one-time passcode (OTP)
  • To display your profile to other Smash users
  • To enable personality-based discovery and matching
  • To deliver real-time messaging between users
  • To process and act on safety reports and blocks
  • To send account-related communications — login codes only
  • To fix bugs and improve app performance
  • To comply with applicable laws and legal obligations
03

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We only share data in these limited scenarios:

  • With other users — your profile name, photos, bio, and prompts are visible to other Smash users as part of the core experience.
  • With service providers — trusted third parties such as Supabase for cloud hosting and email delivery, under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • For legal compliance — if required by law, court order, or to protect the safety of our users.
04

Data Security

All data is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Authentication uses email one-time passcodes — no passwords are ever created or stored. Our backend is powered by Supabase with row-level security enabled. We apply reasonable technical measures to safeguard your data at every layer.

05

Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account via Profile → Settings → Delete Account, we permanently remove your profile, photos, messages, prompts, and credentials within 30 days. This action cannot be undone.

06

Your Rights

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request permanent deletion of your account and all associated data
  • Withdraw consent to any optional data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at libdon4life@yahoo.com.

07

Children's Privacy

Smash is intended exclusively for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we become aware that a minor has registered, we will delete their data immediately. If you believe a minor has registered, please contact us.

08

Community Safety

Smash does not tolerate harassment, sexual exploitation, abusive behavior, or inappropriate content. Users can report or block others at any time directly from any profile. All reports are reviewed within 24 hours and appropriate action is taken, including permanent account removal for violations.

09

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
