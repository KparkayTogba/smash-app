Login & Access 3 questions
How do I sign in to Smash?
Smash uses passwordless login. Enter your email address and we'll send a one-time verification code. Enter that code in the app and you're in — no password ever required or stored.
I didn't receive my verification code.
Check your spam or junk folder first. Wait 60 seconds, then tap "Resend Code." Make sure you entered the correct email address. Still stuck? Contact us below — we'll sort it out.
How do I sign out?
Go to Profile → Settings → Sign Out. You can sign back in any time with your email and a fresh one-time code.
Profile & Prompts 3 questions
How do I set up my profile?
After your first sign-in, you'll walk through onboarding — add your name, age, bio, personality prompts, and photos. Your profile goes live once setup is done.
What are personality prompts?
Short questions you answer to show who you really are — your interests, values, what you're looking for. They help you stand out and give other people something real to connect on.
My photo upload isn't working.
Go to iOS Settings → Smash → Photos and set it to "All Photos" or "Selected Photos." If the issue continues, restart the app and try again.
Safety & Reporting 3 questions
How do I report a user?
Tap the user's profile → tap the three-dot menu (⋯) → select Report. Our team reviews every report within 24 hours and takes action.
How do I block a user?
Tap the user's profile → tap the three-dot menu (⋯) → select Block. They disappear from your feed immediately and cannot contact you.
What violates community standards?
Harassment, hate speech, sexual exploitation, impersonation, spam, abusive language. Report anything that makes you feel unsafe. We take every report seriously.
Account & Deletion 3 questions
How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Confirm when prompted. Your profile, photos, prompts, messages, and credentials are permanently removed within 30 days. This can't be undone.
What data gets deleted with my account?
Everything — profile, photos, prompts, messages, and login credentials. We don't retain personal data after account deletion.
Who can see my profile?
Only other Smash users. We never share your personal information with advertisers or third parties. Period.
