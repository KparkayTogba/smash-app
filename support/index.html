Help Center

Find answers to common questions below, or reach out directly — we respond within 2 business days.

Community Safety

Smash does not tolerate harassment, abusive behavior, or inappropriate content. Report or block any user directly from their profile. All reports reviewed within 24 hours.

Login & Access
How do I sign in to Smash?
+
Smash uses passwordless login. Enter your email address and we'll send a one-time verification code. Enter that code in the app and you're signed in — no password ever required.
I didn't receive my verification code.
+
Check your spam or junk folder first. Wait 60 seconds then tap "Resend Code." Make sure you entered the correct email address. Still having trouble? Contact us below.
How do I sign out?
+
Go to Profile → Settings → Sign Out. You can sign back in at any time using your email and a new one-time code.
Profile & Prompts
How do I set up my profile?
+
After signing in for the first time you'll be guided through onboarding. Add your name, age, bio, answer personality prompts, and upload photos. Your profile goes live once setup is complete.
What are personality prompts?
+
Prompts are short questions you answer to help others get to know the real you — your interests, values, and what you're looking for. They help you stand out and spark better conversations.
My photo upload isn't working.
+
Go to iOS Settings → Smash → Photos and set access to "All Photos" or "Selected Photos." If it continues, restart the app and try again.
Safety & Reporting
How do I report a user?
+
Tap the user's profile → tap the three-dot menu (⋯) → select Report. Our team reviews all reports within 24 hours and takes appropriate action.
How do I block a user?
+
Tap the user's profile → tap the three-dot menu (⋯) → select Block. Blocked users will no longer appear in your feed and cannot contact you.
What violates community standards?
+
Harassment, hate speech, sexual exploitation, impersonation, spam, and abusive language are all violations. Report anything that makes you feel unsafe — we take every report seriously.
Account & Deletion
How do I delete my account?
+
Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. Confirm when prompted. Your profile, photos, prompts, messages, and credentials will be permanently removed within 30 days. This cannot be undone.
What data is deleted with my account?
+
Everything — your profile, photos, personality prompts, messages, and login credentials are permanently deleted. We do not retain your personal data after account deletion.
Who can see my profile?
+
Only other Smash users can see your profile. We never share your personal information with advertisers or third parties.
